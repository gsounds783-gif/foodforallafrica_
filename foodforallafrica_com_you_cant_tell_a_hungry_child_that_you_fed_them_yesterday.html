You can’t tell a hungry child that you fed them yesterday.

School meals are vital in shaping the future of our next generation, and ensuring their success is a shared responsibility.

Our Executive Director, Elijah Amoo Addo is currently in Osaka, Japan, participating in the 24th Global Child Nutrition Foundation Forum (Dec 9-12, 2024). This forum brings together public and private stakeholders to exchange ideas, share experiences, and find inspiration to build and expand high-quality school meal programs worldwide.

At Food for All Africa, we’re proud to showcase our LunchBox School Feeding Initiative while learning innovative strategies for growth and expansion. Together, we can create a brighter future for every child.
